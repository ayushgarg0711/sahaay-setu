Statutory Process

4 Immediate Steps to Legal Protection & Relief

Under the Scheduled Castes & Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and 2016 Amendment Rules:

Step 1

Immediate Zero FIR (Section 4)

Police stations cannot refuse or delay your complaint. An officer who delays an atrocity FIR faces mandatory imprisonment.

Police Emergency: 112
Step 2

Free Medical Exam (Sec 357C)

Hospital examination and Medico-Legal Report (MLC) is 100% free at all government hospitals for injuries or sexual assault.

Women Helpline: 181
Step 3

50% Relief Voucher on FIR

The District Magistrate must disburse first 25% to 50% statutory relief directly to your bank account without waiting for trial.

Relief: ₹85k to ₹8.25L
Step 4

Sec 15A Armed Security & Legal Aid

Survivors and witnesses are entitled to armed police security and a free state-appointed advocate for court proceedings.

NALSA Legal Aid: 15100

Your Rights Under the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act

The law provides strong, non-negotiable guarantees for victims and witnesses of caste atrocities.

Crucial Legal Protections You Must Know

  • Mandatory Registration of FIR: Under Section 4, a public servant who delays registering an FIR commits a punishable offense.
  • Zero FIR Right: You can file an FIR at ANY police station regardless of jurisdiction.
  • Free Legal Representation: Under NALSA, every SC/ST survivor is entitled to a free advocate funded by the state.
  • Statutory Relief: Relief is payable in stages starting immediately after the FIR, independent of conviction.
  • Witness Protection: Section 15A guarantees armed police protection and immediate bail cancellation for accused.

Statutory Relief & Compensation Scales

Offense / Situation Minimum Relief Amount Payment Stages
Rape / Gang Rape ₹5,00,000 to ₹8,25,000 50% after medical exam & FIR; 25% on charge sheet; 25% after trial.
Murder / Death of earning member ₹8,25,000 + Pension / Employment 50% after post-mortem & FIR; 50% on charge sheet. Plus widow/family pension.
Grievous Hurt / Permanent Disability ₹4,25,000 to ₹8,25,000 50% after medical report; 50% on charge sheet.
Arson / Burning of Home Full rebuilding cost + ₹1,00,000 Immediate brick-by-brick reconstruction assistance.
Public Humiliation / Caste Slurs / Boycott ₹1,00,000 to ₹2,00,000 25% on FIR, 50% on charge sheet, 25% on trial conclusion.

What to do if Police Refuse FIR?

  1. Send by Registered Post: Under Section 154(3) CrPC / Sec 175 BNSS to the Superintendent of Police (SP).
  2. Contact DLSA (15100): Free advocate can file a Section 156(3) application before the Magistrate.
  3. National SC/ST Commission: File online at ncsc.nic.in.
